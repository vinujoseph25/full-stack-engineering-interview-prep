## HTML & Accessibility

### Technical Questions

1. How has HTML5 evolved over your career, and what features do you find most valuable?
2. Explain semantic HTML and why it matters for accessibility and SEO.
3. How do you ensure your web applications are accessible? What WCAG guidelines do you follow?
4. What are ARIA roles and when should they be used?
5. Explain the differences between `<section>`, `<article>`, `<div>`, and `<span>`.
6. How do you optimize HTML for SEO?
7. Explain the purpose of the `<head>` section and the essential meta tags.
8. How do you handle responsive images in HTML?
9. What is server-side rendering and client-side rendering? What are the trade-offs?
10. How do you implement proper form validation?

### Experience-Based Questions

1. Describe a complex accessibility challenge you solved in a previous project.
2. How have you mentored junior developers on HTML best practices?
3. Describe how you've implemented progressive enhancement in your applications.

## Preparing Your Own Questions

Always prepare thoughtful questions to ask your interviewers. This demonstrates your interest and engagement. Some examples:

1. What are the team's biggest challenges right now?
2. How is success measured for this role?
3. What does the onboarding process look like?
4. How does the company approach technical debt?
5. What opportunities are there for professional growth and learning?
6. How would you describe the company's engineering culture?
7. What are the next major projects or initiatives for the team?
8. How do you approach work-life balance in the organization?

## Tips for Remote Interviews

1. Test your equipment and internet connection beforehand.
2. Find a quiet, well-lit space for the interview.
3. Have a backup plan for technical issues (phone number, alternative internet connection).
4. Keep notes and reference materials nearby but organized.
5. Practice speaking clearly and concisely.
6. Maintain eye contact by looking at the camera.
7. Dress professionally, even for remote interviews.
8. Be prepared to share your screen for coding exercises.
