# Leadership & Behavioural Interview Practice

## Story bank

Prepare 6–8 reusable stories from real engineering experience.

Recommended coverage:

| Story | What it demonstrates |
| --- | --- |
| Difficult technical decision | Architecture judgement and trade-offs |
| Production incident | Ownership, debugging and resilience |
| Performance improvement | Measurement and optimisation |
| Conflict or disagreement | Influence and communication |
| Mentoring | Technical leadership and people development |
| Challenging delivery | Planning, prioritisation and execution |
| Failure or mistake | Accountability and learning |
| Major change | Adaptability and stakeholder management |

## Answer structure

**Situation → Task → Action → Result → Learning**

Keep the situation concise. Spend most of the answer on the decisions you personally made and the actions you took.

## Make answers senior-level

- Explain why you chose an approach.
- Describe alternatives you rejected and why.
- Show how you influenced other people.
- Quantify business or engineering impact where possible.
- Include what you learned.
- Avoid presenting team achievements as individual achievements.

## Common prompts

- Tell me about a difficult technical decision.
- Tell me about a production incident you owned.
- Tell me about a disagreement with another engineer or stakeholder.
- Tell me about a time you mentored someone.
- Tell me about a project that was at risk.
- Tell me about a failure and what changed afterwards.
- Tell me about a significant architecture improvement.
- Tell me about a time requirements changed late in delivery.
